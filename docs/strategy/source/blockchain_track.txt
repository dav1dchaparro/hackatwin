HackaTwin — Blockchain Track: Rules and Requirements
Oct 16, 2026 · @sofi
Overview
The HackaTwin Blockchain track has a single prize. To compete, every project must meet the mandatory
Avalanche requirements (scored up to 100 points). Integrating 0xGasless or KUMPLY is optional and adds
up to 20 bonus points.
Part Type Points
Avalanche requirements Mandatory Required for eligibility
Jury evaluation Mandatory Up to 100
0xGasless integration Optional Up to +10
KUMPLY integration Optional Up to +10
Mandatory requirements
A project that does not meet all six requirements is not eligible for the track prize, even if it earns bonus
points.
1. Smart contract on Avalanche. Deploy at least one smart contract in the Avalanche ecosystem,
on testnet (Fuji) or mainnet: C-Chain or your own L1. The contract must be verified on the
corresponding explorer. If you create your own L1, it must show measurable P-Chain
transactions.
2. End-user interface. Integrate the contracts with a web or mobile app. A CLI or a bot does not
satisfy this requirement.
3. Why Avalanche. At least one component must justify using Avalanche over another network,
documented in the repository. Valid examples: time to finality, custom L1 configuration,
compliance and privacy for institutional use cases, Interchain Messaging (ICM).
4. Business model. Answer at minimum: what problem it solves, who the customer is, how it
generates revenue and what its competitive advantage is. Any early validation (users, potential
customers, interviews) is a plus.
5. Public repository. With clear installation and deployment instructions and a description of the
architecture.
6. Original work from the event. Libraries, SDKs, boilerplates and prior projects are allowed, but
only work built during the hackathon is evaluated. If you start from an existing project, the
README must clearly separate what was built during the event.
Optional bonus points
Integrating our partners' tools is not required, but it adds points on top of the jury score. Each
integration is scored separately and both can be combined (maximum +20).

Deliverables and scoring
Each team submits a single package. The final score is the jury score (up to 100) plus bonus points from
integrations (up to +20).
Deliverables
• Public repository with a README: installation, deployment, architecture, why Avalanche, and
what was built during the event.
• Verified contract address and explorer link.
• Working demo (URL or video) and a pitch of up to 3 minutes.
• If claiming bonus points: a README section on the integration (0xGasless and/or KUMPLY) with
links to the demo transactions.
Jury evaluation (up to 100 points)
