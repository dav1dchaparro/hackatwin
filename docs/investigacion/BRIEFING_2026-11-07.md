## A. Winning entries, stablecoin / payments / DeFi tracks

## B. What judging actually rewards

- **Five official criteria**, equal-ish weight: Technicality, Originality, Practicality, Usability (UI/UX/DX), WOW factor ([ETHGlobal rules](https://ethglobal.com/events/ethonline2026/info/details)). Same page: 7-minute final pitch — 4 min demo + 3 min Q&A; a mandatory 2–4 minute demo video; max **3 partner prizes** selected, since "it is the only way for partners to assess your project".
- **Sponsor tracks are a second, separate contest.** Partners judge asynchronously and ask for an explicit "how you used our tools + feedback" write-up ([same](https://ethglobal.com/events/ethonline2026/info/details)). Avalanche states its criteria as "value proposition, technical complexity and usage of Avalanche technologies" ([Hack2Build](https://build.avax.network/events/5ce3a8c2-21db-40fa-b40f-f82ecdde99db)).
- **Theme gravity is measurable.** At Cannes, 98% of submissions were agentic and 40% used Circle Nanopayments; most track winners made the sponsor primitive the core, not a feature ([Arc blog](https://www.arc.io/blog/meet-the-arc-track-winners-from-ethglobal-cannes-hackathon-and-what-we-learned)).
- **Patterns in what won**: (1) one sentence, one user, one tiny payment ("$0.01 to the artist", "a receipt"); (2) a live transaction the judge can see; (3) the sponsor's primitive inside the pitch noun phrase (x402, Nanopayments, CRE, WDK); (4) a business line that survives the weekend (Payload Exchange's sponsor, PayMate's credit pool, Onda's escrow); (5) a demo that moves — hardware, a song, two phones with no signal. Removing friction (zkLogin, gas sponsorship, WhatsApp/Chrome) beats infrastructure.
- **HackaTwin's own rubric differs from ETHGlobal's**: Idea / Problem / Business / Pitch, each 1–5, equal weight, averaged over 100; deliverables = public repo, ≥1 on-chain tx hash, working demo, 3-min pitch + 2-min Q&A ([track PDF](track%20twin.pdf), transcribed at [track_twin.txt](track_twin.txt)). No WOW category, no explicit "use of sponsor tech" — but the Idea criterion says the Twin stablecoins must be "at the core of the product".

## C. Twin Finance

- **What it is**: onchain financial infrastructure for LATAM — fully backed local-currency stablecoins plus tokenized global assets ([twin.finance](https://www.twin.finance/)). Stablecoins are issued by **Twin Finance S.A.S.** (Uruguay); tokenized assets by **Twin Finance Bermuda Ltd.** (Class M Digital Asset Business licence) ([transparency page](https://www.twin.finance/transparency)).
- **Tokens**: ARGt, BRAt, COLt, PERt, MEXt, CHLt, BOLt — ERC-20 upgradeable (OpenZeppelin beacon proxy), 18 decimals, on Arbitrum, Base, Polygon, Ethereum and Avalanche ([track PDF](track%20twin.pdf)); per-token explainers exist for [ARGt](https://help.belo.app/en/articles/12833571-what-is-argt), [CHLt](https://help.belo.app/en/articles/14225505-what-is-chlt), [MEXt](https://help.belo.app/en/articles/14226371-what-is-mext), [COLt](https://help.belo.app/en/articles/14226444-what-is-colt), [BOLt](https://help.belo.app/en/articles/14226495-what-is-bolt), [PERt](https://help.belo.app/en/articles/15308441-what-is-pert). Trade press describes "six" local stablecoins ([Bank Magazine](https://bankmagazine.com.ar/twin/)); the hackathon PDF lists seven including BOLt.
- **Backers — correct the assumption**: Tether backs **Belo**, not Twin. Belo raised a **$14M Series A led by Tether** (Mindset Ventures, Titan Fund, The Venture City, G2), with ARGt as its core asset ([Stablecoin News, 30 Apr 2026](https://stablecoinnews.com/belo-raises-14m-in-a-round-with-tether/)). Belo is Twin's main distribution channel; no Twin equity/funding disclosure found [UNVERIFIED]. Founder/CEO appears as **Rafael De Ambrosi** ([podcast listing](https://www.ivoox.com/en/twin-finance-desde-stablecoins-locales-respaldadas-hasta-inversiones-audios-mp3_rf_176946610_1.html)); Twin's site links to Paisanos ([twin.finance](https://www.twin.finance/)).
- **Regulatory history (material for anyone building on ARGt)**: Argentina's CNV ordered a halt to ARGt offering/promotion, then lifted it on 21 Apr 2026 after Belo removed the 32% TNA "remunerated accounts" messaging and Twin added corporate disclosures, a whitepaper and non-security warnings — while the CNV still asserts ARGt is a *valor negociable* under a Howey-style test ([Cointelegraph ES](https://cointelegraph.es/news/cnv-de-argentina-levanto-suspension-sobre-la-stablecoin-argt); original order [here](https://cointelegraph.es/news/argentina-la-cnv-ordeno-suspender-la-oferta-y-negociacion-de-la-stablecoin-argt)).
- **Docs & addresses**: whitepaper [TwinStablecoins_Whitepaper_v1.pdf](https://cdn.twin.finance/docs/TwinStablecoins_Whitepaper_v1.pdf); reserve reports 30 Sep 2026 and 10 Jun 2026 ([transparency](https://www.twin.finance/transparency)). Contract addresses (tokens, Morpho vaults on Arbitrum, Curve ARGt/USDT0 pools, LayerZero OFT bridge adapters) are in the track PDF — e.g. ARGt `0x59863989d080B22476DB95656d0C3CC18be92214` on Arbitrum, vault `0x9Dd3F844747AB78d616BF76DB92756E17A064aDD`. **No smart-contract security audit was found** [UNVERIFIED]; the reserve reports are accounting attestations, not code audits.

## D. Onchain credit, RBF/MCA, ROSCA, LATAM payments

- **Income-backed credit**: **Cred-Fi** — "income-backed, under-collateralised credit lines for Humans and AI Agents", ETHGlobal Buenos Aires showcase ([ethglobal.com](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/cred-fi-tguaw)).
- **Revenue-based financing**: **Strevvo** — "finance real-world business revenue through AI-validated, tokenized on-chain assets orchestrated by CRE", Chainlink Top 12 ([chain.link](https://chain.link/hackathon/winners/strevvo)). **FlowVault** — autonomous yield allocator, 1st DeFi ([chain.link](https://chain.link/hackathon/winners/flowvault)). **Ghost Finance** — private P2P lending with sealed-bid rates, 2nd DeFi ([chain.link](https://chain.link/hackathon/winners/ghost-finance)).
- **Merchant cash advance / e-commerce receivables**: **Dow Protocol** raised $10.5M to finance e-commerce merchants against receivables ([CoinMarketCap/CoinDesk excerpt](https://coinmarketcap.com/community/articles/6a759d6546ffa177d96053f5/)) — [UNVERIFIED] details.
- **ROSCA / tanda / pasanaku**: **PasaTanda** — "AI-driven latinamerican group savings on WhatsApp", savings circles as Sui objects, zkLogin, yield on idle USDC, HackMoney 2026 ([ETHGlobal showcase](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/pasatanda-c3f0w)). **MiTanda** on DoraHacks ([dorahacks.io](https://dorahacks.io/build/44307)). **Pasacoin** ([showcase](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/pasacoin-ywv02)).
- **LATAM remittance / payroll stablecoins**: **Efy Finance** (Panama; founded 2019 Barranquilla) — started as receivables financing for importers/exporters, pivoted to cross-border settlement; raised **$5M led by Tether** on 5 Nov 2026; five core markets Colombia, Mexico, Bolivia, El Salvador, Panama ([TechNode Global](https://technode.global/2026/11/05/panama-based-efy-finance-raises-5m-seed-round-led-by-tether-to-expand-latin-american-payments/)). **Takenos** — LATAM stablecoin account/remittance network, $7M seed extension ([LatamList](https://latamlist.com/takenos-raises-7m-seed-extension-to-expand-across-latin-america/)). **Belo** — consumer wallet + Mastercard + QR, ARGt rails ([help.belo.app](https://help.belo.app/en/articles/12833571-what-is-argt)).

**Implication for a Twin-track build**: every credit winner above is USD-denominated, so *local-currency* credit and group savings is the open lane — e.g. scoring a merchant's on-chain collection history and repaying in ARGt/BRAt, or a tanda settled in local currency without promising yield (see the CNV history) — both inside the track's own suggested ideas ([track_twin.txt](track_twin.txt)).
